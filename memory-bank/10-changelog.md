- 2026-11-13 · Qoder · F-46 · **The AI's payment read now runs under an identity of its own.** The audit graded F-46 partial — *"AI investigation gated by grant (server.ts), but distinct role not confirmed"* — and the feature's own technical note asks for a hard boundary: *"AI service account has read-only access to payment logs."* The gating was real (F-107's `ai_investigation` capability, the zero-retention gate, the minimised projection, the sensitive-read audit) but the identity behind the read was still the platform's own: `public.ai_payment_log` was a `SECURITY DEFINER` function owned by the platform, reading the payment tables with the platform's rights. `supabase/migrations/20261112130000_f46_investigation_only_role.sql` gives the AI a role of its own — `investigation_only`, `nologin`, `nosuperuser`, `nocreatedb`, `nocreaterole`, `noinherit`, a member of no role and with no members — and makes it powerless: two minimised views in `app_private` are the whole of what it may read, and the two reads it **owns** (`app_private.investigation_read`, `app_private.investigation_event_read`) run with its rights, so a line that touched a payment table would be refused by Postgres rather than merely discouraged.
