## Problem & Why

Nigerian celebration planning fragments budgets, vendor negotiations, family preferences and commitments across disconnected tools. Hosts struggle to compare offers and recover from failures; vendors lack consistent quoting, coordination and trust infrastructure.

Ówàńbè connects culturally contextual planning, vendor commerce and event execution while keeping commitments and money under human control. Cultural guidance must acknowledge variation rather than prescribe universal Yoruba practices.

## Goals and Non-goals

**Goals:** Produce actionable plans; support comparable quotes and accountable bookings; protect funds and sensitive information; improve vendor operations; support diaspora hosts and weak-connectivity venues.

**Non-goals:** Autonomous AI financial authority; host delegation; unrestricted staff access; universal cultural authority; global event ERP; invented availability, benchmark prices or verification claims.

## Target Users & Personas

- **Host:** Plans celebrations or books for another person; owns decisions and reviews.
- **Vendor owner:** Manages services, quotes, team, subscriptions and payouts.
- **Vendor staff:** Performs explicitly delegated business tasks.
- **Guest/door operator:** Claims invitations or validates admission with minimal access.
- **Support, finance, operations, arbitrator:** Resolve cases through separated permissions.
- **Assigned event manager:** Coordinates recovery for assigned events only.

## User Stories: Core Workflows

1. **Host planning:** Capture intent/budget → review sourced suggestions → save approved plan.
2. **Negotiated booking:** Compare providers → chat → counter → accept vendor revision → acknowledge risk → pay → receive confirmed allocation.
3. **Vendor operations:** Owner configures services/staff → staff quotes within permissions → owner controls payout changes.
4. **Completion/dispute:** Host confirms completion or files timely evidence; system releases or freezes the remaining balance accordingly.
5. **No-show:** Host submits evidence → support triages → alternatives appear → human approves recovery advance.
6. **Guest admission:** Guest claims single/couple slot → presents card → operator validates without invitation-edit rights.

## Edge Cases & Failure States

- Duplicate/out-of-order callbacks must not duplicate charges, allocation, refunds or releases.
- Late payment, quote revision and simultaneous acceptance require transactional checks against current versions.
- Full processor outage preserves remaining window duration; recovery must not restart a fresh 24 hours.
- Refund failure remains pending and escalated, never falsely shown completed.
- AI/provider outage preserves drafts and exposes retry/manual paths.
- Offline scanners can conflict; display stale-cache warnings and reconcile duplicate admissions.
- Revoked links/access must invalidate online operations; offline revocation limitations require explicit policy.
- Failed recovery, unavailable replacements and insufficient recovery funds require human escalation without guarantees.
